# Surface: InfantiWeb app (child + family)

Mode: Operate (child app is task-led play; family panel is a reading dashboard).
Audience: children 6-10 on phones; families checking a weekly summary.
Constraints: PRODUCT.md accessibility floor; Figma Make prototype "Replicar prototipo en Figma" is the approved composition reference.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm lilac playroom where every screen asks one thing and shows the way back. Refuses the dense edtech dashboard and the hyper-saturated cartoon arcade.
OWN-WORLD: lilac ground #F7F4FF, deep-violet ink #3B2F63 for text and the single action color #5B4BA0; emotion colors only on emotions; Nunito everywhere; thick violet outlines on flat vector art; 16-24px rounded cards with 2px soft borders; drawn SVG icons, never emoji.
STORY: the child sees Nubi, knows where to continue, plays one question at a time, is never punished, and sees stars and badges grow; the family reads practice, not diagnosis.
FIRST VIEWPORT: welcome = logo top-left, big headline and two 64px role buttons left, Nubi floating in a lilac disc right with speech bubble; on phones stacked with buttons full width.
FORM: Figma prototype structure kept, with an SVG winding path on the map where Nubi walks to the current station as the signature interaction; motion = soft rise on enter, star pop on success. seed: none (pinned) "para que te des una idea de como hacer el frontend".
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
